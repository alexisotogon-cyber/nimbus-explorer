# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nimbus Explorer serves two audiences in the same FinOps decision flow:

- Finance and FinOps leaders need a fast, defensible view of cloud spend, savings, reconciliation, confidence, and next actions.
- Cloud and engineering teams need progressive access to the evidence, assumptions, metrics, commands, remediation, and reversal steps behind each opportunity.

Both groups work from the same uploaded billing evidence. The primary job is to decide what to investigate or change next without losing financial traceability.

## Product Purpose

Nimbus Explorer turns AWS, Azure, GCP, and FOCUS billing exports into a deterministic, auditable cost-optimization report. Success means that a user can understand the financial result, verify the evidence, adjust scenarios, and hand off an action plan without relying on AI for calculations, translation, validation, or exports.

Atlas is a guided explanation layer over those deterministic results. It may explain findings and answer open questions, but it must never become the source of financial truth.

## Brand Personality

Expert, serene, and precise. Nimbus should feel like an experienced FinOps analyst at a decision table: calm under scrutiny, explicit about uncertainty, and concise enough for leadership without hiding technical evidence.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-dashboard appearance or ornamental “AI magic”.
- Nested cards, repeated boxes, excessive rounding, and decorative gradients.
- Square icon containers used by default.
- Tiny text, truncated financial values, or layouts that waste half the viewport.
- Simultaneously exposing formulas, assumptions, simulators, and commands.
- Decorative motion, bouncing controls, or scale effects that move surrounding content.
- Green used for generic actions instead of financial meaning.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before persuasion.** Every conclusion exposes its source, coverage, and confidence.
2. **One financial truth.** Dashboard, Atlas, scenarios, PDF, Excel, and Markdown use the same deterministic result.
3. **Executive first, technical on demand.** The first screen supports a decision; progressive disclosure supports verification and execution.
4. **Calm density.** Use the available viewport efficiently without becoming visually noisy.
5. **Safe next action.** Recommendations distinguish verification, remediation, and reversal before a user acts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA across light and dark themes. Support keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, 200% zoom, screen-reader announcements, accessible dialogs and tabs, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not communicate status by color alone. The complete product, Atlas, errors, and exports must work deterministically in Spanish and English.
